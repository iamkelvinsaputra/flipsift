Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

In short: FlipSift runs entirely in your browser. It has no account, no analytics and no ads. Everything it saves stays on your computer. The only time it contacts a server is to check a Pro license key with our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy. We never sell data, and we never see your browsing.

Who this covers

This policy covers the FlipSift browser extension (“FlipSift”) and this website. “We” means the developer of FlipSift.

What FlipSift reads

FlipSift only runs on Facebook Marketplace pages (addresses starting with facebook.com/marketplace). On those pages it reads what is already on your screen: listing titles, prices, locations, and labels such as “Sponsored”. It uses them to decide which listings to hide or fade, and to fill in the flip calculator.

FlipSift does not read your messages, profile, friends, posts or anything outside Marketplace. It never clicks, posts, sends messages or changes anything on your account.

What FlipSift saves, and where

Everything below is saved with your browser’s own extension storage on your computer. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not synced between computers.

WhatWhyHow long
Your settings and blocked wordsTo filter Marketplace the way you choseUntil you change them or remove FlipSift
IDs of listings you’ve seen or opened, with the timeTo fade or hide listings you’ve already seenThe most recent 30,000; older ones are deleted automatically
Resale prices and platforms you type for a listingSo the calculator remembers them when you returnThe most recent 3,000
Your buy log: item title, listing link, prices, platform, statusSo you can track and export what you bought (Pro)Until you delete entries or clear the log
Your Pro license key and its statusTo unlock Pro featuresUntil you deactivate Pro or remove FlipSift

You can clear your seen history and your buy log at any time in FlipSift’s settings. Removing FlipSift from your browser deletes all of it.

What FlipSift sends

FlipSift makes one kind of network request: checking a Pro license key with Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com). This happens when you activate a key, about every 12 hours while Pro is active, and when you deactivate it. Each request sends:

  • your license key,
  • an ID for this installation, created by Lemon Squeezy when you activate, and
  • when activating, a name for the installation, such as “FlipSift on Chrome (2026-11-15)”.

No browsing data, listings, settings or buy log is ever included. Free users’ copies of FlipSift make no network requests at all. Lemon Squeezy handles these requests under its own privacy policy.

Buying Pro

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record for FlipSift Pro. It collects your payment details, email address, billing country and tax information at checkout, and handles them under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. Lemon Squeezy shares your name, email address, country and order details with us so we can send your license key, provide support and handle refunds. We use them only for that, and keep them for as long as the law requires for sales records.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

FlipSift’s use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. FlipSift uses data only to provide its single purpose: filtering Facebook Marketplace and calculating flip profit. We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use it for any purpose unrelated to FlipSift’s features.

Children

FlipSift is meant for adults buying and reselling goods, and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If FlipSift ever starts collecting or sending anything new, we will update this page before the change ships and change the date at the top.
