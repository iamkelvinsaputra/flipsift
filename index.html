Questions

Is FlipSift made by Facebook?

No. FlipSift is an independent tool. It isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meta, Facebook, eBay or any other platform it mentions.

Does it do anything on my Facebook account?

No. It only changes what Marketplace pages look like in your own browser, the way an ad blocker does. It never sends messages, clicks buttons, posts or changes anything on your account.

Does it work outside the US?

Yes. FlipSift works on Marketplace in any country and reads prices in any currency, including £, € and Rp. The built-in fees cover US and UK selling platforms, and you can add your own.

Does it work on my phone?

Not yet. It’s a browser extension for Chrome on a computer. It also works in Edge and Brave.

How do I cancel Pro Monthly?

Use the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email to manage or cancel your subscription. Pro keeps working until the end of the month you paid for.

Can I use Pro on more than one computer?

Yes, on up to 3 browsers. To move it, choose “Move Pro to another computer” in FlipSift’s settings, then enter your key on the new one.

Facebook changed something and FlipSift stopped working

Facebook changes its pages often. Updates usually reach you automatically within a few days. You can also tell us what you see.
